# 🛒 Retail Sales Dashboard | Data Analysis & Visualization

> **Overview:** An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing retail sales performance, customer demographics, and product category trends to drive data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective
To analyze retail transaction data and extract actionable insights regarding:
- **Sales Performance:** Total revenue, transaction volume, and average sales value
- **Customer Demographics:** Age group preferences and gender-based purchasing patterns
- **Product Categories:** Sales distribution across Electronics, Clothing, and Beauty
- **Temporal Trends:** Monthly sales patterns and daily transaction fluctuations

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
| Category | Tools |
|----------|-------|
| **Data Analysis** | SQL (Aggregations, Filtering, Grouping) |
| **Visualization** | Power BI (Interactive Dashboards, DAX Measures, Slicers) |
| **Data Modeling** | Star Schema Design, Relational Database |

---

## 📊 Key Findings

✅ **Sales Performance:**
- **Total Sales:** $456K across **1,000 transactions**
- **Average Transaction Value:** $456 per transaction
- Strong revenue generation with consistent transaction volume

✅ **Category Analysis:**
- **Clothing** leads with $156K (34.12% of total sales)
- **Beauty** follows closely with $157K (34.41%)
- **Electronics** contributes $144K (31.47%)
- Balanced distribution across all three categories indicates diverse product demand

✅ **Customer Demographics:**
- **Age Group 40-49** shows the highest transaction volume
- **Age Group 50-59** represents the second-largest customer segment
- **Age Group 20-29** and **30-39** show moderate engagement
- Gender distribution appears relatively balanced between Male and Female customers

✅ **Temporal Insights:**
- **February** emerges as the peak sales month
- **May, July, and December** show strong performance
- **September** records the lowest sales volume
- Daily transaction patterns reveal operational trends for staffing optimization

---

## 💼 Business Impact & Recommendations

🎯 **For Marketing Teams:**
- Focus targeted campaigns on the **40-59 age demographic** as they represent the core customer base
- Develop gender-neutral marketing strategies given the balanced customer distribution
- Leverage peak months (February, May, December) for major promotional events

🎯 **For Inventory Management:**
- Ensure adequate stock levels for **Clothing and Beauty** categories as they drive the majority of revenue
- Plan inventory replenishment cycles aligned with monthly sales trends

🎯 **For Operations:**
- Analyze daily transaction patterns to optimize staffing schedules
- Implement loyalty programs for high-value age groups (40-59)

🎯 **For Strategic Planning:**
- Investigate the dip in September sales and develop counter-strategies
- Expand product offerings in top-performing categories to maximize revenue
